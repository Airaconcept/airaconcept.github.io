A. 带排名版（需要 awk / sort）

关小火箭，复制粘贴回车。列：下载Mbps 上传Mbps 延迟ms 域名，最后按下载排序。

U=https://speed.cloudflare.com
D="stores.staples.com www.sage.com openai.com guide.for.edu.sg www.giannidelprete.it www.akasantech.com staticdelivery.nexusmods.com www.leics.police.uk www.dbs.com.sg www.blibli.com www.hypixel.net www.speedtest.net www.trumpinternationalrealty.com www.udacity.com www.carousell.sg m.iyf.tv www.sloomb.com chrono24.com store.ubi.com www.shopify.com www.okta.com www.visa.cn"
[ -n "$1" ] && D="$*"
curl -s -o up.bin "$U/__down?bytes=10000000"
: > r.txt
echo "下载Mbps 上传Mbps 延迟ms 域名"
for d in $D; do
  p=$(curl -s -m 5 --connect-to ::$d:443 -w '%{time_starttransfer}\n' -o /dev/null -o /dev/null -o /dev/null "$U/__down?bytes=0" "$U/__down?bytes=0" "$U/__down?bytes=0" | sort -n | head -n 1)
  dl=$(curl -s -o /dev/null -m 10 --connect-to ::$d:443 -w '%{speed_download}' "$U/__down?bytes=25000000")
  ul=$(curl -s -o /dev/null -m 10 --connect-to ::$d:443 -w '%{speed_upload}' --data-binary @up.bin "$U/__up")
  echo "$p $dl $ul $d" | awk '{printf "%7.1f %7.1f %6.0f  %s\n",$2*8/1e6,$3*8/1e6,$1*1000,$4}' >> r.txt
  tail -n 1 r.txt
done
echo "===== 按下载排名 ====="
sort -rn r.txt

B. 纯 curl 版（不支持管道时用）

每行输出：域名 连接秒 下载B/s 上传B/s。Mbps = B/s × 8 ÷ 1000000。

curl -s -o up.bin 'https://speed.cloudflare.com/__down?bytes=10000000'; for d in stores.staples.com www.sage.com openai.com guide.for.edu.sg www.giannidelprete.it www.akasantech.com staticdelivery.nexusmods.com www.leics.police.uk www.dbs.com.sg www.blibli.com www.hypixel.net www.speedtest.net www.trumpinternationalrealty.com www.udacity.com www.carousell.sg m.iyf.tv www.sloomb.com chrono24.com store.ubi.com www.shopify.com www.okta.com www.visa.cn; do curl -s -o /dev/null -m 10 --connect-to ::$d:443 -w "$d %{time_connect} %{speed_download} " 'https://speed.cloudflare.com/__down?bytes=25000000'; curl -s -o /dev/null -m 10 --connect-to ::$d:443 -w '%{speed_upload}\n' --data-binary @up.bin 'https://speed.cloudflare.com/__up'; done
